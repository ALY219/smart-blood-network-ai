SMART BLOOD NETWORK OFFICIAL DONOR GUIDELINES

1. AGE AND WEIGHT REQUIREMENTS
All whole blood donors must be between 18 and 65 years old and weigh at least 50 kg (110 lbs). Donors aged 16-17 may donate with parental consent depending on local regulation.

2. HEMOGLOBIN AND HEALTH SCREENING
Prior to donation, hemoglobin levels are checked. Minimum hemoglobin required is 12.5 g/dL for females and 13.0 g/dL for males. Pulse must be regular between 50 and 100 bpm, and blood pressure must be within normal ranges.

3. DONATION INTERVALS AND RECOVERY
The minimum interval between whole blood donations is 56 days (8 weeks) for men and women. For double red cell donations, the waiting period is 112 days (16 weeks). Platelet donors can donate up to 24 times a year, waiting at least 7 days between donations.

4. COMPATIBILITY & SPECIAL CONDITIONS
O-negative is universal red blood cell donor type. AB-positive is universal plasma donor. Pregnant individuals or those who have given birth in the last 6 months are deferred from donating whole blood.
