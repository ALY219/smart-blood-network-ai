SMART BLOOD NETWORK EMERGENCY PROTOCOLS & POST-DONATION FAQS

1. EMERGENCY RESPONSE TIME AND DISPATCH
When an emergency blood request is submitted through the app, eligible nearby donors are notified within 3 minutes. Donors must confirm availability within 15 minutes to secure rapid hospital transport.

2. POST-DONATION CARE AND RECOVERY
After donating blood, rest in the waiting area for at least 10 to 15 minutes. Drink plenty of fluids (at least 4 extra glasses of water) over the next 24 hours. Avoid heavy lifting, strenuous exercise, or operating heavy machinery for at least 12 hours post-donation.

3. BLOOD COMPONENT SHELF LIFE
Whole blood and red blood cells can be refrigerated for up to 35 to 42 days. Platelets must be stored at room temperature with constant agitation and expire in just 5 days. Fresh frozen plasma can be stored frozen for up to 1 year.

4. RARE BLOOD TYPES
The Bombay Blood Group (hh) is an extremely rare blood type lacking the H antigen. Individuals with the Bombay blood type can only safely receive red blood cells from other Bombay blood group donors.
